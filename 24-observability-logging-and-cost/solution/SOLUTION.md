# Solution for Lesson 24: Observability: logging, tracing a call, and knowing your costs

Redesign: the default return becomes a page of summaries (id, name, stage, score, band, one line need preview) with a default page size of 25, a total count, and a cursor or offset parameter, which is the shape `list_leads` grows in the capstone. Detail moves to the existing per lead resource (`leaddesk://leads/{id}`) or a `get_lead` style call for the few records the model actually needs. Description sentence: 'Returns one page of lead summaries (25 by default) with the total count; fetch a single lead's full record before acting on it, rather than listing everything.' The cost logic to state in your answer: full records for 900 leads enter the model's context and are carried through every later turn, while summaries answer the triage question at a fraction of the tokens and the detail fetch stays rare and deliberate.
